*2026-09-26. Covers the fourteen attempts (September 13–24) and the model programme that followed (Attempt B, September 24–26). ## The story in one page

**Where it started.** Event Density is an ontology: the world is made of *becoming* before it is made of things, and time only runs one way. An earlier phase of work had tried to derive all of physics from it and produced around 250 papers, which in the end mostly relabelled known physics in ED's vocabulary. The restart asked a harder question: **what does ED actually establish, when it is tested honestly?**

**Phase 1 — the fourteen attempts (derivation).** Each attempt fixed its meanings in advance, wrote its expected results down before running anything, and kept every miss. They asked ED to *derive* things, above all space itself. Four solid results came out, and one wall: **ED says what may happen, but not where a new event goes**, so it never produced space from nothing. Every one of the fourteen attempts hit that wall from a different direction.

**The turn.** Two realisations changed the programme. First, **ED's own founding statement says it supplies "the conditions of possibility, not the full catalogue of outcomes"**, and it declares three-plus-one dimensions as an input. Failing to derive space is not a failure of an ontology that never claimed to. Second, asking "did we make space?" chose the definition of success before the system was understood. So the method changed: **build a model, measure what it does, and interpret last.**

**Phase 2 — Attempt B (models).** First, one more serious try at making space, with better instruments and five new rules. All closed, each for a specific, recorded reason. Then the question changed: **take space as given, as the ontology declares, and ask what ED's own ingredients do in it.** That produced a coherent little world, described below.

## Part 1 — what the fourteen attempts established

1. **Untunability.** A well-developed theory of quantum spacetime (causal dynamical triangulations, CDT) has three numbers it tunes by hand. **ED's conserved budgets fix all three**, with nothing tuned. In 2+1 dimensions the point they fix sits where space holds together. In 3+1 it is unresolved, and one published number decides it. *This is the result the public repo is named for.*
2. **Below three dimensions, clocks can't stay in step.** In one and two dimensions, the effort needed to keep a pattern's clocks together grows without limit as the pattern grows; from three up, it stays nearly level. This was checked against the obvious objection (that it reads the number of connections rather than the dimension) and survived. It matches established physics of coupled clocks, so it rules out one and two dimensions from ED's own content.
3. **Handedness can't be written into mirror-symmetric rules.** This is a proved theorem, with a script that checks it. If the world has a handedness, the state picked it, not the laws. And handedness is possible at all only because time runs one way.
4. **ED carries a dimension it is given; it doesn't make one.** Sixteen careful runs, measured with ED's own definition of dimension, never produced one from a start that had none.

Smaller findings: patterns fall apart below about five connections per event; ED's space can't be a regular grid (gravity would depend on direction); in the 2+1 runs ED never collapsed. Also ruled out along the way: several dark-energy mechanisms, several simpler gravity budgets, and "costs" as a way to prefer extended space.

## Part 2 — Attempt B

### A last serious try at making space (closed)

- **Rules that reward local structure** (loop counts, thickening, local flatness) **all prefer clumps.** A clump is where every such count is highest.
- **Uneven bandwidth ("thin links cost more") works as a measure of distance.** On a shape with a known answer, it recovers a lattice's true size through shortcuts, provided they are about five times thinner. It was the new idea that could, in principle, get past the "everything is close" wall.
- **Memory walkers** (a pattern carrying a fading sense of direction) made things closer, not further apart.
- **"Space is only the present"** produced a tangle that fakes three dimensions on one measure. Checking its shape exposed it.
- **Lesson kept permanently:** how distance grows with size cannot, on its own, tell real 3D space from a tangle.

### Space given: what ED's ingredients do (B-3 to B-12)

**The setting.** A 3D grid. Becoming flows from dense to thin. Committed becoming slows the clocks around it. Becoming commits above a threshold, and committed becoming dissolves after **ħ**, "how much memory a pattern can carry before it dissolves", one fixed number for the whole substrate. New places keep being born. Commitments use up a budget. Participation carries a polarity: a phase.

**What happens, in order of discovery:**

1. **Structure has a switch.** Clumps form when *interaction × commit rate × ħ × threshold* is above **1**. This was worked out on paper first, then confirmed exactly at two different ħ.
2. **The only memory is the present state.** A clump is wherever its stuff is. Remove the stuff and the clump is gone; move the stuff and the clump follows.
3. **Your ink picture, in full.** Structure appears, then ends, either **from outside** (expansion thins it) or **from inside** (spending uses up its budget): uniform → structure → uniform.
4. **A law for when structure dies.** Derived with nothing fitted, it predicts lifetimes under thinning, **including at a rate it was never built on.** It is exact for weakly interacting patterns and bracketed for strongly interacting ones, which shelter each other and die together.
5. **ħ shelters structure,** more the harder structure is driven. Under spending it lengthens structure's life, roughly (not exactly) as a square root.
6. **Grains and exclusion.** Made of grains, the ink keeps the same behaviour. **At most one grain per place forbids structure entirely**, because structure works by piling up.
7. **Patterns together.** A strong pattern **hosts** weak ones, which then live 20–30 times longer, even ones too weak to exist alone. Two strong patterns **merge** and each outlives its lone self.
8. **Polarity.** In step, patterns **bind**. Out of step, they **cancel and part**, and destroy each other's structure (a resemblance to matter meeting antimatter, noted and not claimed).
9. **Polarity is selected by the surroundings.** A pattern survives only if its phase is close to the stuff around it (within about a quarter turn).
10. **The One Being.** Structure condensing out of a uniform, single-phase start is fully aligned and runs the whole arc. **Alignment stays whatever it was at the start**, so a coherent beginning is what makes structure possible at all.

## How strong each result is

Not all results carry the same weight, and they shouldn't be quoted as if they did.

- **Proved or exact:** the handedness theorem; untunability in 2+1 (exact algebra); the structure switch at 1.
- **Laws, tested beyond the data they came from:** the death law for weakly interacting patterns; the clock floor, checked under a control it never had.
- **Clean, reproduced observations:** the memory result; the full ink arc; hosting and merging; binding and parting; selection by surroundings; alignment inherited from the start; exclusion forbidding structure.
- **Approximate:** ħ's sheltering trend; the square-root lifetime under spending; the grain model's switch, which depends on how the smooth model was translated into grains.

## How it was done, and why it can be trusted

- Meanings were fixed in writing before models, and **the author decided every meaning**: distance, the One Being, blinking events, memory, and ħ.
- **What would be measured was fixed before each run, not what would count as success.**
- Every instrument was checked on known answers first, including a null.
- **Two working sessions reviewed each other**, and many of the best catches came that way.
- **About twenty claims were withdrawn or narrowed**, including some within an hour of being made. Each withdrawal is kept with its reason. Every correction made the record more precise, and none of the final results depends on a withdrawn one.

## Where ED stands now

- **What it is:** an ontology, with its inputs stated openly (the Born rule, the area law, 3+1 dimensions, a starting shape).
- **What testing established:** it fixes what another theory tunes (untunability); it rules things out (one and two dimensions, handedness in symmetric rules, a grid substrate); and, with space given, its own ingredients produce a coherent world of structure, memory, hosting, binding and parting, and a return to uniformity.
- **What it doesn't do:** make space; say where a new event goes; produce a new measurable prediction. It doesn't claim to.
- **The open questions, now sharper:**
  1. Can anything in ED change a medium's polarity after the start, or is it only inherited?
  2. Is the polarity connection (P05) a field of its own?
  3. The exact lifetime laws in the hard cases.
  4. Where a new event goes: still the deepest one.

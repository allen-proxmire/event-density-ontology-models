# Results from models: what ED's ingredients do in supplied space

*Allen Proxmire, 2026-09-24 to 29. A companion to [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md), the results from analysis and proof, which are the ones to read first.*

**These seventeen results show what ED's own ingredients do when run in supplied three-dimensional space. They are behaviours of the rules, not claims about nature, and each carries its own strength and scope.**

* How the work was done is in [../method/HOW_IT_WAS_DONE.md](../method/HOW_IT_WAS_DONE.md); the code for every result is in [../method/models/](../method/models/).*

---

## The setting

ED's ontology declares three-plus-one dimensions as an input; it does not claim to make space. So these models **supply space as a 3D grid and ask what ED's own ingredients do in it.**

The ingredients, and nothing else:

- **possibility** — what has not yet committed — flows from concentrated toward diffuse;
- committed becoming slows the clocks around it;
- possibility commits above a threshold, irreversibly at that moment — and that commitment is **becoming** (in the author's meaning, becoming *is* commitment: the tick, the update; what is not committing is not becoming anything);
- committed becoming dissolves back into possibility after **ħ**, read (the author's meaning) as *how much memory a pattern can carry before it dissolves* — one fixed number for the whole substrate;
- new places keep being born (expansion);
- commitments use up a budget (spending);
- participation carries a **polarity**, a phase (primitive P09), transported with the stuff (P05).

In the author's reading, **the rate of commitment is the local clock**: committing and dissolving tick at the local clock rate, as flow does, while new places arrive on one shared beat. Results 1–11 were first measured with committing and dissolving on a common clock; result 12 records what changes, and what does not, when they run on the local one.

**These are models, not derivations.** They show what the ingredients do, not what nature does. Each result is scoped to the rules, sizes and settings run.

## The results

**1. Structure has a switch, and the switch has a formula.** Lasting structure forms when *interaction strength × commit rate × ħ × threshold* exceeds **1**. The value was derived on paper first — the background density cancels from the algebra — and then found exactly there in the runs, at two very different ħ, with nothing tuned. It holds unchanged when committing and dissolving run on the local clock (result 12). A spreading drop needs somewhat more (about 1.6–2.1), because clumping has to outrun the spreading. *Strength: derived, then confirmed under both clock rules.*

**2. The only memory is the present state.** Remove a clump's committed stuff and it re-forms on the spot. Spread its free stuff away and it never returns. Move that stuff and the clump follows it. Nothing stores where a clump was — as the ontology says, *nothing accumulates a record of itself.* *Strength: direct test, three seeds.*

**3. Structure is temporary, and ED supplies both of its endings.** Left alone, clumps sustain themselves indefinitely. Expansion — new places being born — thins them from outside until they cannot rebuild. Spending — commitments using up a budget — makes structure burn its own budget from inside. **Either way the whole arc appears: uniform, then structure, then uniform again**, the ontology's picture of every structure as a temporary attractor (§13).

**With committing and dissolving on the local clock (result 12), the arc is unchanged in kind and the two endings change in weight.** Under expansion it appears in every case, and structure returns completely to uniform. With both endings it runs in 4,200–5,600 steps, with about 90% of everything thinned away and only 1–4% spent. **Under spending alone it still heads home, but far more slowly** — where the same setting ended at 31,800 steps with committing off the clock, it was still going at 300,000 and declining throughout, more than nine times longer and not yet finished; one case that did finish took 265,500 steps against about 6,000. Dense regions spend on their own slowed clock, so they burn their budget slowly. *Strength: observed in every run, under both endings and both clock readings, with the off-the-clock runs reproducing their original figures to within the sampling resolution.*

**4. How long structure lasts, estimated from the rules — two regimes, two formulas.** Both are derived from the rules with nothing fitted, and both predict the life *remaining after* structure's peak.

**Isolated clumps, which leak away on their own.** Five quantities measured at the peak give the remaining life to within about a factor of two. This is the weakly interacting case.

**Many clumps holding the medium up together.** The clumps sustain the possibility around them far above what thinning alone would leave — by factors of tens to millions — and give it up slowly, like a reservoir draining to hold a level. A formula built on that picture, **one measured input and nothing fitted**, gives the remaining life to **within about 15%**: `T = ((1+κħ)/3h)·ln((N³+s·C₀)/(N³+s·C_end))`. Eight cases fall inside its range, and **four of them it had never seen** — 0.93, 0.98, 1.04 and 1.14 against the truth, under both clock rules, with the formula frozen and each case fixed in advance of its run. This is the strongly interacting case, and the first quantitative account of it.

**Where each applies.** The collective formula holds for κħ between about 2 and 4, expansion no faster than 10⁻⁴ per step, and a background still above the switch level when structure peaks. Above κħ of about 10 it over-predicts; under faster expansion it under-predicts; where the background has already fallen to the switch level at the peak, the clumps are effectively isolated and the first estimate is the one to use. **Both edges are the same quantity:** where the possibility around the clumps sits relative to the switch level while structure declines — held above it the formula over-predicts, dragged below it by fast thinning it under-predicts, and sitting at it the formula is accurate, which accounts for its error in 30 of 31 cases to within about a sixth. Those edges were mapped by testing against them, and they sit closer in than first drawn. *Strength: the collective formula is derived, frozen before testing, and accurate to about 15% on unseen cases inside a stated range — the most accurate lifetime estimate here; the switch (result 1) remains the one exact result. The range itself is empirical. The isolated-clump estimate is derived and good to about a factor of two; with committing on the local clock (result 12) those clumps sit in a halo of their own possibility and barely leak, which that estimate does not include.*

**5. ħ shelters structure, more the harder it is driven.** Against thinning, the sheltering grows with how hard structure is driven: absent right at the switch and reaching 2.6× at the strongest setting. Under spending, a longer ħ lengthens structure's life, roughly as a square root.

**With committing and dissolving on the local clock (result 12) the pattern keeps its shape and gains a sign change at the bottom.** Measured across five drive strengths, long memory against short gives 0.74, 1.08, 1.37, 1.62 and 1.98 — so the sheltering still rises steadily with drive, reaching about 2× at the strongest setting, but **just above the switch a long memory slightly shortens structure's life rather than lengthening it**, and the benefit only begins a little further up. Under spending the square-root law is out of reach on this rule, because the runs outlast the 300,000-step cap; what can be read is that longer memory still holds more structure at the cap (0.06, 0.14 and 0.33 of everything still committed for ħ of 10, 30 and 100), which is the same direction as before. *Strength: empirical and reproducible, on both clock readings; the off-the-clock runs reproduce their original figures to within the sampling resolution. The square-root law is established only for committing off the clock.*

**6. Grains keep the behaviour; exclusion forbids it.** Made of discrete grains, the ink keeps the same switch and the same runaway clumping, and the switch falls steadily as grains get denser. With **at most one grain per place, structure never forms**, because the amplifier works by piling up and one-per-place forbids the pile. *Strength: observed; the grain switch's exact value depends on how the smooth threshold is translated into grain counts.*

**7. Strong patterns host weak ones, and merge with each other.** A weak pattern that dies almost at once on its own lives 20–30 times longer inside a strong pattern's clumps, even when it is too weak to form structure alone. Two strong patterns merge, and each outlives its lone self. Slowed clocks belong to the place, so a strong pattern's slowing shelters everything nearby. *Strength: observed, with lone controls.*

**8. Polarity decides whether patterns bind or part, and is selected by the surroundings.** With participation as a complex amplitude, √bandwidth × e^{i·polarity} (P09), **patterns in step bind even more strongly, and patterns in opposite phase cancel each other's slowing where they meet, separate, and both die early** — a resemblance to matter meeting antimatter, noted and not claimed. At a quarter turn the result reduces exactly to simple addition, an internal consistency check. **Each pattern's fate is set by its phase relative to the stuff around it:** within about a quarter turn of its surroundings it keeps its structure, and beyond about half a turn it cannot (16 of 17 drops). This is the ontology's reading of polarity as relational — "chain-in-environment" — seen as behaviour. *Strength: observed, pre-registered.*

**9. What structure needs from polarity: local agreement at commitment.** Where committing preserves phase, the medium's phase is inherited from the start: structure condensing out of a uniform, single-phase field (the ontology's One Being) is fully aligned and runs the whole arc, holding its alignment to three digits throughout. The ontology's own commitment primitive, P11, goes further — commitment randomizes phase, which is ED's account of decoherence. With P11 in place, **structure forms and lasts when commitments agree in phase with a common local target**, and then it does so from any start. The agreement itself is what does the work: a filter blind to phase, rejecting exactly as often, does nothing. Agreement and quantity both matter — tightening the window raises agreement (0.63, 0.90, 0.97) but costs commitments, and structure needs enough of both. The local target can be the medium's own phase, a fixed constant or a frozen random pattern; all three work alike, so what counts is **local agreement itself**. *Strength: observed, pre-registered, with a matched phase-blind control; seeds agree on lifetime and to within 0.004 on peak.*

**10. After the start, a medium orders itself — and one rate decides whether it does.** Under P11 with local agreement at commitment, **neighbouring regions come into phase over time**: agreement between adjacent regions rises from about 0.7 to between 0.93 and 0.99 over a run, on both seeds. And the whole medium follows: from a scrambled start, essentially all committed stuff comes to share **one** phase, reaching within 1–2% of full alignment by about 4,000–5,000 steps, while a fifth to a quarter of everything is still committed. **This order is produced, not supplied** — the filter is never told what to order toward. It is the first behaviour in this programme that makes a medium more ordered than it started. It arrives after structure has peaked, so it accompanies structure's decline rather than preceding it.

**In the author's reading, polarity changes only at commitments.** Becoming is commitment, so a pattern's phase is set when something commits and is otherwise carried unchanged: it does not turn between ticks, and it is tied to the local clock because commitment is the clock. That is the reading under which the medium orders itself. The models also show why continuous turning would not fit: turning a phase steadily with the local clock leaves the ordering standing only while it stays well below 1/ħ, and at 1/ħ the ordering inverts, with structure's lifetime unchanged either way. Route dependence is real and small for anything passing through a clump — about a hundredth of a turn, one clump-site's worth of slowing, with a same-route control reading exactly zero; the large effect is on what *stays* in a slowed place. *Strength: observed, pre-registered, two seeds agreeing on direction and size.*

**11. Where a new event goes costs little, once space is given.** ED does not say where new possibility appears, and uniform thinning — the models' default — is itself one answer: new places appear evenly everywhere. Four readings from the ontology's own text were compared: evenly everywhere; where things are densest; where clocks run fastest; and at commitments (*every event enters the universe through a commitment*). Each removed exactly the same total per step, matched to one part in 10¹⁵, with its pattern both live and frozen.

**The choice costs at most about a fifth of structure's life and changes nothing else measured.** Where things are densest costs 11–17%; at commitments 17–23%; where clocks run fastest, nothing measurable. The full arc survives under every reading, the whole-medium ordering of result 10 still reaches its ceiling, and clump counts move by about 5%. **So in supplied space, ED does not need to answer "where" in order to work.**

**Why placement washes out is a matter of rates.** Clumps are constantly swapping possibility with their own committed store, about 400 times faster than thinning removes anything, so whatever thinning takes from a clump is topped up at once from its reserves. **A clump feels how much new space arrives, not where.** "Evenly" put about 64% of its thinning at clumps and "fastest clocks" about 33%, and the clumps came out the same to within about 1%. "At commitments" is a distinct statement from spending (result 3): with spending off in both, it still shortens structure, 10,000 steps against 13,000 and 35,000 against 44,000. *Strength: observed, pre-registered, two seeds agreeing on lifetimes. No reading is ruled in or out: a reading that leaves structure alive longer is not thereby the true one. Scope: one grid size, two base models; this asks where new capacity appears within supplied space, not whether new events build space.*

**12. When the rate of commitment is the local clock.** Committing and dissolving gain the local clock rate — commit at `κ·r·(ρ−θ)`, dissolve at `c·r/ħ` — so every process at a place runs on that place's clock, while new places arrive on one shared beat.

- **The switch is unchanged.** The clock rate multiplies both sides of the commit/dissolve balance and cancels, so the steady committed amount is unchanged; the same cancellation leaves the relation between slow, long-range disturbances untouched and only slows their timescale. Measured: no structure at 0.8 or 0.9, structure at 1.1 and 1.25, at two ħ, exactly as under the common clock. **Result 1 holds under both readings of the clock.**
- **Dense places run slow.** Clumps grow and merge along the same path about four times more slowly, so at any given time there are more of them, each less dense.
- **Structure is ended from outside.** Spending works through dissolving, and dense places now dissolve slowly, so they spend slowly. With spending the only ending, structure is still alive at 150,000 steps with 20–30% of its budget used. With both endings, life doubles in one base model (18,000 → 36,000) and lengthens in the other (9,000 → 11,000). **New places arriving becomes the main way structure ends.**
- **Dense places fall behind slightly when new space is born elsewhere.** Placing new space where clocks run fastest leaves dense regions fuller than placing it evenly — at every sampled time, in both seeds and both base models, growing to about 3–6% late in structure's life. The direction never varies; by the bar fixed before the runs (three times the seed scatter), it is clear on one measure in one base model. The same small effect is present with a common clock, and here it has longer to build.
- **Both halves of the rule matter.** Committing on the local clock with dissolving off it gives weak, short-lived structure (6,000 steps against 18,000); with both on it, structure is as strong as before.

*Strength: the switch, derived and confirmed at two ħ, is the strongest thing here; the slowed growth and the ending balance are observed with two seeds agreeing closely; the falling-behind is consistent in direction and small in size. Scope: one grid size. Structure forms only where clocks slow a great deal, so these models do not show a gently slowed region that holds structure.*

**13. Polarity's order is made by commitment, and handed back to possibility.** Possibility — what has not committed — does come into step in these worlds: from a fully scrambled start its coherence rises from 0.004 to about 0.35 over a run. **But commitment gets there first.** By step 100, **a third of everything has already committed** while possibility's coherence is still 0.009, and structure peaks at step 600. The mechanism is visible in the rules: commitments are laid down in agreeing phases (result 9), and when committed becoming dissolves it hands that phase back to possibility. **The order in possibility is recycled commitment.**

**And commitment does not merely build that order — it sets it.** Order supplied at the start is drawn *down* to the same level: a partly-in-step beginning starts at 0.61, falls to 0.34 within a hundred steps, and settles where the scrambled start arrives from below. Possibility's coherence is an attractor fixed by commitment, approached from above as readily as from below, whatever the world began with.

**The alternative was tested and does nothing.** ED's connection (P05) transports polarity between neighbouring places; if it also made possibility flow more readily between places *in step*, possibility could in principle come into step on its own before anything committed. At full strength it produced **no early order at all** — at structure's peak possibility's coherence is 0.035 against 0.052 without it, so slightly lower. Structure peaked at step 600 in **every** arm, so nothing was merely delayed into looking early.

**What this says about ED.** Order is not laid down first with structure built upon it. **It is made by events happening, and spreads back into possibility as settled things dissolve** — order is something that has happened, not something waiting in advance, which is what the ontology says about time. *Strength: observed, pre-registered, two seeds agreeing on every reading's direction and exactly on structure's timing, with the timing guard passed exactly (structure's peak at 600 in all fourteen runs). Scope: one form of phase-sensitive transport at two strengths, on result 10's base model. The other route by which order might come first — uncommitted possibility slowing clocks — does not arise: in the author's meaning uncommitted possibility is not becoming, and only becoming slows clocks.*

**14. Committed polarity acts as a field, and its reach grows as possibility spreads.** In the author's own account of fields (*Fields and Forces in Event Density*), nothing in ED is a field of its own: a field is the coarse-grained summary of a stable pattern in how participation is organised, and a force is the bias such a pattern puts on where things go. **Committed polarity does exactly that.** Place a patch of aligned commitments in a scrambled medium and both possibility and newly committed becoming around it lean toward the patch's phase, **and the lean falls away smoothly with distance, still measurable thirteen sites out.**

**The comparison that makes it a field rather than a presence.** A patch of any commitments disturbs its surroundings — it has density, slows clocks, consumes possibility. So the aligned patch is measured against one identical in every respect *except that its internal phases are scrambled*, and only the difference counts. That difference runs about 0.6 at the patch, 0.15–0.3 four to five sites out, 0.04 at ten sites and 0.01–0.02 at thirteen — a profile, not a contact effect. **And the instrument reads zero where it must:** in a medium already in step, where there is nothing to bias, aligned and scrambled patches differ by at most 0.005 beyond three sites.

**The reach belongs to the medium, and it grows.** Change the patch's volume twenty-sevenfold and the reach moves by about a quarter — 2.7–2.9 sites for the smallest, 3.3–3.4 for the middle, 3.4–3.7 for the largest — so the length is a property of the surroundings and only mildly of the source, which is what makes it a field rather than a shape. **And it widens with time**, because the influence is passed along: the patch dissolves, its phase spreads, new commitments take it up and dissolve in turn. Across every reading the length tracks **how far possibility has had time to spread since the patch appeared** — about 0.46·√(D·t), holding to within a fifth over a sixteenfold range of times and memories, where a length set by memory alone would be out by a factor of four. **That is close to plain spreading, not half of it:** ordinary diffusion from a held source, measured by the same method, gives 0.51–0.56, so the field spreads about a tenth more slowly than possibility would on its own. **Short memory caps it:** with memory cut to a quarter, the reach stops growing early and falls below that line. Later the field narrows again as the whole medium comes into step by itself (result 13).

**And it needs its source.** Nothing outlasted the patch: with the patch left to dissolve, one run held its profile for a while and the other was overtaken by its surroundings within about 500 steps with no profile forming at all. The patch changed nothing about the world at large — structure's life and peak are the same with it as without. *Strength: observed, with the pre-registered control passing and the instrument null passing; the reach is reproduced across seeds at early times and its growth with spreading time holds across three memory lengths and three patch sizes, which is the firmest part of this result. The seeds diverge on the reach at late times, so that is given as a direction and not a number; the released-source runs are not reproducible, so the only claim drawn from them is that nothing outlasts its source; and the instrument's null is looser at long memory (up to 0.05 rather than 0.005). Scope: the patch is placed by hand, so this shows what such a region does and not that ED makes one — result 13 speaks to that; one patch size, one grid size, on result 10's base model.*

**15. How far a mass's influence reaches.** A mass's load ripples outward and thins over the sphere, and clocks slow wherever it reaches. In ED's reading — the load is a **trace** that committed matter imposes on its surroundings, not a committed thing itself, so dissolution after ħ does not apply to it — **the load falls off as 1/r, with no cutoff.** Clock slowing then goes as 1/r and the pull as 1/r², which is gravity's weak-field form.

**Said plainly: that form is what spreading gives, not what ED adds.** A steady source spreading something conserved through three dimensions must fall off as 1/r — it is the solution of Laplace's equation outside a source, the same equation Newtonian gravity obeys. So this shows the ontology's picture is **consistent** with gravity, and it is not a derivation of it. What the runs do establish is that the picture works as written: the falloff exponent comes out **identical for a tenfold change of mass**, so it is a clean power law whose size scales with the mass and whose shape does not.

**Kept on file, as the reading ED does not take.** If the load *did* dissolve like committed becoming does, the reach would be finite, and its length would be **√(D·ħ)** — measured at **3.13 and 6.18** sites for ħ of 100 and 400, against 3.16 and 6.32 from the arithmetic, and the same for a tenfold mass. **That is the same length that sets how far the polarity field reaches (result 14)**, so a single length would govern both. The reach would also grow only **weakly with mass** — roughly as its logarithm, adding some six to eight sites per tenfold mass at ħ = 400, which is about half to two-thirds of that length per natural log of mass. Memory would set the scale and mass would barely move it.

**And discreteness on its own does not end the reach.** Load arriving in whole units that cannot be split does not stop at the place where a share falls below one unit: it piles up there until it tips over, and the front moves on. A sharp edge needs dissolution as well as discreteness. *Strength: observed; the 1/r form is the generic consequence of spreading in three dimensions and is reported as such; the conditional length matches its arithmetic to within 2% and is mass-independent, which is the firmest number here. Scope: one grid size, two values of ħ, mass spanning three decades; the logarithmic slope is approximate and drifts upward with mass, so it is given as a range.*

**16. Space is a necessary input, not a convenient one.** The ontology declares its space: P06's statement is that *the substrate's spatial axis is ℝ³, plus one time dimension*. **The question these models asked is whether the rest of ED could produce it anyway** — and the answer is that it cannot, which is what makes the declaration load-bearing rather than a shortcut.

**Nine models grew worlds from events alone, with no space supplied.** Parents chosen for maximum difference (contrast); bandwidth required to balance at every event; new possibility entering at the threshold; parents required to be neighbours in the structure grown so far; and these in combination. **They produce strands, tangles and small worlds, and never a consistent set of directions** — which is what space is, and what makes east-then-north land where north-then-east lands. Locality came closest: paths rejoined and the structure stayed even everywhere, two of the three things space needs. But the rejoining held at a steady 40% instead of climbing toward certainty, and a steady fraction is arithmetically incompatible with the growth a space has.

**Why that is a result and not nine failures.** The corpus has a rule for its own primitives: one earns its place only by forbidding something the others cannot. **Contrast was put through that test and failed** — every operational guise of it turned out to be supplied elsewhere, so it was demoted from a primitive to a heading. **Space has now been put through the same test, by model, and passed.** Take the spatial primitive away and the remaining twelve do not rebuild space.

**What that means for how ED should be read.** Declaring the number of dimensions is the normal situation: general relativity does not derive four, the Standard Model does not derive its gauge group, and causal dynamical triangulations has to tune to reach four. ED declares 3+1 openly, and now has evidence the declaration is **necessary** rather than convenient — which is a check most frameworks have not run on themselves. *Strength: nine models, each with its observables fixed in advance, on three calibrated instruments (how fast a neighbourhood grows with distance, how often routes rejoin, and how often a long walk returns) each checked first against flat spacetimes of known dimension. Scope: the routes tested are the four named above and their combinations; the question of whether some other route could succeed stays open, and is recorded that way. One further reading was costed and set aside: directions that almost commute would give three dimensions from two, with the third made by their failure to commute — but that space is uneven at large scales in a way the real one is not.*

**17. ED has two ways to cut a region off, and the clock wins.** The ontology can isolate a region either by **breaking its links** — bandwidth collapsing to zero, so nothing can cross — or by **stopping its clock**, since committed matter slows clocks and everything at a place happens at that place's rate. Both happen. They compete, and when the mass keeps growing the clock wins outright.

**The sealed region, which exists while the region is fed.** Bandwidth collapse makes a genuine cut: a region of **exactly** zero bandwidth, with nothing crossing in either direction. It is **sharp** — from zero to measurable within about one place — **sized by the mass**, and **the same size at every box size and grid fineness** that was tried. This is the “decoupling surface” the ontology's own notes ask for, and the two-way character is what that means: a region out of contact, not a one-way door. (This is ED's answer to whether it makes horizons: a horizon lets things in but not out, and ED's ingredients make neither that nor an exact permanent cut, but a region out of contact while fed, and a clock that slows without limit.)

**What makes it sharp is reading the collapse as following commitment, not load.** The ontology's notes say bandwidth collapses where things get extreme, without saying which quantity sets it; the models read that two ways. A mass's load falls away smoothly with distance and has no edge anywhere, so a collapse driven by it has none either — and its size then turns out to be set by the edge of the simulated world rather than by the mass. **Commitment only happens above a threshold**, so it stops at a definite radius that the mass fixes. Reading the trigger as commitment is what turns a soft, box-sized dip into a sharp, mass-sized surface, so this half of the result also says what the ontology's collapse rule would have to mean for the surface to exist.

**But the seal cannot hold itself up.** Once the region's supply depends on its own seal, it starves: possibility stops arriving, commitment stops, the drain stops, and the bandwidth heals back. What is left is a very deep but **leaky** dip — bandwidth near the mass about a ten-thousandth of normal, never exactly zero — and it holds steady rather than opening and closing. **Letting the mass grow does not rescue it; it undoes it.** Possibility does pile up inside, unable to commit, but the clock there slows about thirty times faster than the pile-up grows, so commitment nearly stops, the drain dies, and the links heal open.

**What deepens instead is time.** As the mass grows the region's clock slows without limit and with no floor, running hundreds of times slower than outside by the end of a run. **It slows gently, as a power of time** — the exponent came out at about 0.67 and 0.55 at two commitment rates, steady from early in a run to late, so it is a clean power and not a runaway. Nothing ever freezes: a region like this gets ever closer to being cut off and never arrives. *Strength: observed, with the key argument settled on paper before the runs (a slowed-clock trap is a well, not a seal, since anything that gets in gets back out) and with the quantity that decides the outcome written down in advance. The fed surface is exact, reproduced across three box sizes, two grid finenesses and two masses. The clock law's exponent is stable from the first half of a run to the second, unchanged by halving the time step, and unchanged by whether committed matter dissolves. Scope, and it matters: the seal exists **while the region is fed**, and the mass grows without limit only **while the surroundings are held full** — that unlimited growth belongs to the supply, not to the object.*

## Where the models touch established physics

These are resemblances, not claims.

- Matter gathering where clocks run slow is how ED describes gravity, and it is what the models do.
- Settled structures surviving while the possibility between them thins resembles bound galaxies in an expanding universe.
- Complexity rising and then falling, only when the parts interact, matches Aaronson, Carroll & Ouellette (arXiv:1405.6903).
- The clumping mechanism has a known cousin, motility-induced phase separation (Cates & Tailleur 2015).
- Opposite-polarity patterns cancelling each other's structure resembles matter meeting antimatter, which ED's concept files identify with opposite polarity. Possibility and becoming together are conserved throughout; what is destroyed is the patterns' ability to hold together.
- Dense regions falling behind while new space arrives elsewhere on a shared beat resembles the author's picture of black holes in an expanding world; the models show the direction, at small size.

## Open questions for the ontology

1. **What sets how high possibility sits around a clump?** It governs both edges of the lifetime estimate (result 4), and two attempts to derive it have not predicted it. *The older form of this question — whether the polarity connection is a field of its own — is answered: nothing in ED is a field of its own, and committed polarity acts as an emergent directional field with a statable reach (result 14). Its time component also carries real curvature sourced by ED's own slowing (result 12).*
2. **Exact lifetime laws** under spending and under strong interaction, and a lifetime estimate that includes the halo of result 12.

# Gravity as accounting: why a clock near a mass runs slow

*Allen Proxmire, 2026-10-06. A plain-language account of one result. The measurements are in [../results/EVENT_LAYER.md](../results/EVENT_LAYER.md), results 24 and 25; the ontology they come from is in [../ontology/Event_Density_An_Ontology.md](../ontology/Event_Density_An_Ontology.md), §8.*

---

> **A place near matter is not being pulled on. It is going without.**

That is the whole paper. The rest is what it means and what was measured.

---

## 1. There is only so much becoming to go around

Event Density starts from activity rather than from things. The world is a web of places, and what happens at a place is a **commitment** — something becoming settled that was not settled before. Nothing is permanent: a commitment holds its place for a while, then dissolves back into **possibility**, which is simply what has not settled yet.

**So nothing persists by sitting still.** A thing stays what it is by committing again, and again. A ball resting on a table is not idle; it is being re-made continuously, and if it stopped it would not sit there — it would stop existing.

**And there is only so much to go around.** Possibility is finite and it is shared. A place draws on it to commit, spends it, and what it spends returns to the whole rather than to its own doorstep. **That is the entire economy: one budget, spent locally, replenished globally.**

### Empty space is a quiet neighbourhood

A place in empty space commits in fits and starts. It needs a partner — a commitment is something two places do *together*, never one place acting alone — and it needs a unit of possibility to spend. Often one or the other is missing, so it waits. **Measured, roughly half the places in empty space are committed at any moment, and about two thirds are holding nothing in reserve.** Quiet, intermittent, unhurried.

### Matter is the plant that never shuts down

A knot of matter is different in exactly one way: **it cannot afford to wait.** Its structure is held by particular commitments to particular partners, and the moment one of those dissolves the knot is briefly open. If it stays open, the knot comes undone and the matter is gone. So a place hosting matter re-commits the instant it can.

**That is all matter is, in this account: a pattern that must keep committing to stay what it is.** Not a lump of something. A standing demand on the local supply of becoming.

> **And it follows that mass is a rate, not an amount.** A heavier thing is not a bigger pile — it is a higher duty cycle, a pattern that has to work harder and more often to remain itself. **Which also explains why mass is hard to push around.** A place cannot take up new relations while it is in the middle of a commitment; rearranging happens in the gaps. Empty space is mostly gaps, so it is fluid. A pattern that re-commits the moment each commitment dissolves has almost no gaps at all. **A pattern is stiff because it is busy.**

## 2. The deficit, and the clocks

**Nothing about geometry was put into the model.** No metric, no curvature, no field equation, no force law, no term describing attraction. The rules are the ones above: commit in pairs, spend a unit of possibility, hold the place for a while, dissolve, return what was spent to the whole. **Then a knot of matter is dropped into an otherwise even medium, and the only thing it does differently is refuse to idle.**

**A shortage forms around it.** The knot is consuming possibility faster than its surroundings, and its neighbours are left with less. The shortage is deepest inside the matter and falls away outward.

**And the clocks follow the shortage.** A clock, in this account, is nothing but the count of a place's own commitments — how many turns it has had. **A place short of possibility gets fewer turns, so its clock runs slow.** Read raw against a matched control, with no correction of any kind:

| where | the clock runs |
|---|---|
| in the places hosting matter | **12–47% slow** |
| the surface, part matter and part space | **11–14% slow** |
| one step outside | **4.65% slow** |
| then outward, step by step | **2.25 → 1.14 → 0.57 → 0.30 → 0.15%** |
| beyond that | the background rate |

**Six steps, roughly halving each time, out to three times the width of the matter itself.** Same sign in every run, at every box size, at every level of possibility. *(The outward profile is one setting quoted together rather than assembled from several: two units of possibility per place, possibility moving freely, a box of 30, matter of radius 3. How deep the well is where the matter sits depends on how fast possibility moves — 39% when it moves slowly, 12% when it moves freely, because a better-fed structure starves less.)*

> **This is gravitational time dilation with nothing geometrical behind it.** There is no pull in the model. There is a queue for a finite resource, and the places next to the heaviest consumer are at the back of it. **A clock near matter is not being bent. It is waiting its turn.**

**One more thing falls out, and it is not what you would guess.** What matter spends returns to possibility *at large*, not to its own neighbourhood. So the far field ends up slightly **richer** than the control, by one to three per cent, and those distant clocks run a fraction *fast*. **A mass starves its neighbours and feeds the world.**

## 3. The shape is spreading's, not ours

The shortage around matter does not just fall off — it falls off in a particular shape, and in a rich medium that shape is the Newtonian one. Matched against the exact solution for a drain of that size in a box of that size, with nothing fitted:

| steps out | measured | exact |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 0.674 | 0.677 |
| 3 | 0.469 | 0.468 |
| 4 | 0.332 | 0.332 |
| 5 | 0.230 | 0.230 |
| 6 | 0.153 | 0.147 |
| 7 | 0.092 | 0.084 |

**Agreement to within 0.7%, at every speed of possibility and both box sizes.**

**It would be easy to overclaim this and the honest reading is less exciting.** Anything that is conserved, and that spreads in three dimensions, and that is being drained at a point, falls away like this. It is Laplace's equation, and the ontology already derives it in those terms: a mass's reach spreads over a sphere, the sphere's surface grows with the square of the distance, so the reach thins with the square of the distance. **The inverse-square law is what three-dimensional spreading does. It is not evidence for Event Density, and it was never the claim.**

> **What is Event Density's is the drain.** Nothing in diffusion tells you there should be a sink at all. The sink is here because matter must keep committing to exist, and because what it spends goes back to the whole instead of back to itself. **Those two facts are the ontology's, and together they make a mass a standing hole in the supply of becoming.** The shape of the hole is mathematics everyone already had. **That there is a hole, and why, is the result.**

## 4. Where this account runs out

**There is a limit in it, and it is structural rather than a matter of tuning.** It showed up as soon as the reach was measured properly, and it is the most interesting thing to come out of the work.

**Two things turn out to be the same thing.** How strongly a clock responds to a shortage, and how fast the medium closes that shortage back up, are both set by one quantity: how sensitive a place's rate of becoming is to how much possibility it has.

So the account has two regimes and they pull apart:

| the medium | the shortage | the clocks |
|---|---|---|
| **rich** | spreads far, unscreened, the exact Newtonian shape | **barely notice** — about 3% per unit of shortage |
| **lean** | closed up within a step or two | **react hard** — 36% or more per unit |
| **between** | screened at about five steps | enough of both: the six-step well of §2 |

> **A medium whose clocks notice a missing unit of possibility is, for that very reason, a medium that replaces it quickly.** A medium that lets a shortage spread for ever is one whose clocks have enough in hand not to care. **Reach and strength are two faces of one quantity, and this mechanism cannot have both.**

**Gravity has both.** It reaches without limit and it is felt. So **either the reach or the strength comes from something this account does not yet contain** — and saying so is more useful than claiming the problem away.

**What has been tried.** Everything above runs on the simplest version of the rules: one commitment at a place at a time. The ontology does not say that — a place has several distinguishable channels — so the model was run with places free to commit on several at once. **It did not get past the limit.** In a rich medium the clock rose by about a third and stopped just short of the single-commitment ceiling, and it became no more sensitive to a shortage. **What caps becoming there is no longer the one-at-a-time limit but the model's own step for pairing places up:** each place offers to one neighbour per tick and commits only if the offer is returned. **That step is the model's, not the ontology's.** The trade-off stands, and whether the pairing step is where it is decided has not been tested.

---

## What this paper claims, and what it does not

**It claims:** that a pattern which must keep committing to persist is a net consumer of becoming; that this produces a measurable shortage around it; that clocks in that shortage run slow, read raw, by amounts that fall away smoothly over several steps; that mass in this picture is a duty cycle rather than a quantity, which also accounts for its stiffness; and that the whole of it emerges from rules containing no geometry.

**It does not claim:** that the inverse-square shape is evidence for the ontology — it is what spreading does; that matter's persistence was derived — it is a rule, and the clock well is the consequence of that rule being paid for; that space was produced — which pairs of places may be neighbours is handed to every model here; or that gravity has been accounted for, since the trade-off in §4 says plainly that this mechanism cannot yet deliver reach and strength together.

**And one limit worth stating on its own:** a bigger mass does not reach further. Across matter from radius 2 to radius 5 the well does not widen — **what sets the reach is the medium, not the mass.** That is not how gravity behaves, and it is unresolved.

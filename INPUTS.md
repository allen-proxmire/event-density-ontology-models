# What was put in, result by result

*Every result in this repository is listed here with what it was given. A result that needs a rule we added is marked so, in the same row as the result. Nothing is summarised away: the three results tables carry the detail, and this page is the index to who supplied what.*

**Read this before the results.** It is the only way to tell which findings follow from the ontology and which follow from a rule written to make them happen. **Some of each are here.**

## 2. Supplied in every result, everywhere in this repository

**The Born rule, the area law, 3+1 dimensions, and a starting shape.** Named as inputs on the front page.

**Every knot and every loop is seeded.** No structure in this repository organised itself out of the medium.

**Matter's persistence is a rule, not a finding.** A matter pattern's internal links are reserved for their own partners, so it cannot stop committing. The clock well is then a consequence of that rule.

## 7. What is not here

**No prediction a measurement could contradict.** Stated on the front page and in `results/RESULTS.md`.

**The clock well is short-ranged; the shortage behind it is not.** A place's blink rate saturates with the possibility available, so the rate responds over about two places while the shortage itself reaches as far as the box shows. See `SCOPE.md`.

**No inertia, no momentum, no metric, no field equation.** A pattern in these models drifts while something biases its next commitment and stops when that stops. **So nothing here produces an orbit or a free fall**, and that is a property of the rules rather than a stage not yet reached. See `SCOPE.md`.

**No external review.** Nothing in this repository has been read by anyone who works on the frameworks it touches.

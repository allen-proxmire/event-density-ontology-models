# Event Density

*Allen Proxmire.*

**Event Density (ED) is an ontology** — an account of what the world might be made of, under physics. It starts from one conviction: **time only runs one way.** Once something has happened, it can't be undone.

It is a theory of possibilities. It is not a theory of gravity and not a theory of everything. It asks a different question: *what must be true for anything to have a structure at all?*

**What this repository contains** is that account, a set of discrete models built from its rules, and what happened when they were run exactly — thirty numbered results, each with its own strength and scope, with the code. **The models are lattices of fifteen to thirty places with no inertia, no metric and no field equation, and nothing here has been compared with an observation of the world.**

## What came out, and what was put in

**Two of the three findings that carry the most weight need nothing beyond the neighbour relation — which places may be neighbours — supplied to every model here.**

1. **Clocks run slow at the bottom of a well.** Matter has to keep committing to stay what it is, so it spends the possibility its neighbours would have used — and in an engine with **no metric and no field equation in it**, that alone slows the clocks around it, deepest where the matter sits and recovering shell by shell outward. **A place near matter is not being pulled on. It is going without.**
2. **A region that began first stays permanently older.** Time here is the count of commitments, not a background everything slides along — so a head start cannot be repaid. Start all the possibility in one ball and let it spread: within a few hundred blinks **every rate matches a world that began uniform**, and the place where becoming began ends **about 5 blinks older.** The rates equalise; the age does not. **The relic is permanent at every speed of possibility; how big it is depends on how long possibility has to spread**, and the figure above is at the engine's standard setting.

**The third needs a rule written to produce it, and it is the one that most resembles physics.** The steering weight that makes matter draw matter together, and the strength of that weight, are **ours**; without it, loops are pushed apart. The equal response of different-sized patterns is in part a property of averaging a centroid. **Both are reported here as that** — see [INPUTS.md](INPUTS.md) §6.

3. **Free matter gathers, and the pull does not care how big the body is.** Matter's own clocks run slow, so its cadence falls behind, and that lag spreads outward — a second consequence of the same spending, still being measured and held in the working record rather than published here. Steered by it, closed loops of 12 and 24 links are pulled **at the same rate — 1.04 ± 0.03 across four couplings** — matching what the bare grid gives. **The response of extended matter to a phase gradient does not depend on its size.** At weak coupling a loop is still pushed **away** — and that holds between two free loops in an otherwise empty box, with no knot, no walls and no apparatus of any kind, so **the pushing is a property of the rules rather than of the test setup.** **Above a coupling of about 0.11 for loops of 48 links and 0.19 for loops of 24, the sign changes and free matter is drawn together.** Measured in three pairings — two 48s, two 24s, and a 24 beside a 48 — the net motion is inward in **all six cells tried, by between 2.7 and 6.7 standard errors**, with nothing held and nothing pinned. **And the heavier pair does not merely approach — it settles:** two loops of 48 sit **6.2 ± 1.2 closer than their own bodies alone would put them**, within ten places **78% of the time**, holding a standoff of **6.9 ± 0.2**. **So the pull is established, the pushing is established at weak coupling, and above it the gathering is established too.** *What this still is not: free fall.* What is measured is **two bodies drawing together, not a test body falling through a source's field**; it is a rate of **drift, not an acceleration**; and **the coupling is the model's own parameter rather than a derived number.** **The steering rule is declared, not derived:** the ontology says direction is carried by phase, and this model makes that concrete by having each place lean toward whichever neighbour is running behind. **That rule sits on top of the primitives rather than following from them**, so the gathering is what the rule does — **it does not fall out of the bookkeeping on its own.**

**And one result reaches outside ED — with a negative verdict in the dimension that matters.** three quantities that causal dynamical triangulations leaves free and tunes by hand are **fixed by the ontology** — exact algebra, in any dimension, at a viable point in 2+1 and at a point measured to lie outside CDT's semiclassical phase in 3+1. **The dictionary that makes the comparison possible is ours, and is recorded as a set of decisions rather than derivations.**

## The idea

* **A discrete web, not a smooth fabric.** Reality is not a container that things sit inside. It is an enormous web of places and the relations between them, and **nothing about it is continuous.** The web keeps growing: new places are continuously being born.
* **The blink *is* the time.** Each place has its own cadence — a **blink**: one commitment, held for a while, then let go. A clock ticking, an atom persisting, a ball sitting still: none of these is something happening *in* time. **The blinking is what time is**, and a clock is nothing but a place's count of its own blinks.
* **The ledger only runs one way.** What hasn't committed yet is **possibility** — unsettled, quantum, trying many paths at once. Each blink settles a piece of it into fact, and **once settled it cannot be unsettled.** Time has a direction because a spent event cannot be un-happened.
* **And the count leaves a mark that cannot be washed out.** If time is the *count* of commitments rather than a background everything slides along, then **a region that began becoming first has simply counted more — and nothing can repay it.** Measured (result 22): pile all the possibility into one ball, let it spread, and within a few hundred blinks every rate matches a world that began uniform — in-step share, clock, how the possibility is spread, all of it. **And the place where becoming began ends permanently older, by about 5 blinks.** The rates equalise; the age does not — and the lead is permanent at every speed of possibility, while its size depends on how long possibility has to spread.
* **Possibility is the fuel, and there is only so much of it.** Every commitment is paid for out of it, by both places taking part. It is conserved — but **what is spent returns to the whole rather than to the place that spent it**, which is what leaves a busy region poor and the far field slightly rich.
* **Matter is a consumer, not a substance.** A particle is a closed knot of relations that must re-commit constantly just to remain what it is — it cannot afford to idle, because a thing that stops committing stops existing. **So mass is how hard something has to work to stay itself**: a rate of becoming rather than a quantity of stuff. It is also why mass resists being moved — **a pattern is stiff because it is busy.**
* **Clocks near matter run slow because they are going without.** Matter draws down the possibility around it, and a place short of possibility gets fewer turns. **This began as a claim of the ontology and is now measured in ED's own rules** (results 24–25): slow where matter sits, recovering shell by shell outward, with no metric and no field equation anywhere in the model. **A place near matter is not being pulled on. It is going without.**
* **Gravity is an economy of events, and it took two mechanisms rather than one.** The slowing is measured. **Starvation alone pushes the wrong way:** a free pattern near matter is pushed *away*, because a commitment is paid for by the place it lands on and the places beside matter are the ones with nothing spare. **What the ontology says should bend a path inward is phase, not plenty — and steered by the cadence lag, free matter is drawn together** above a coupling of about 0.11 to 0.19, with the heavier pair settling at a standoff instead of drifting apart. **So the inward motion comes from the phase the ontology named, not from the shortage.** Two things are still missing: **a body falling through another's field** rather than two bodies gathering, and **a reason the steering rule should take the form it does** — the ontology says direction lives in phase, but the rule that puts it to work is added, not derived.

**On what the models are:** they are discrete lattice models with **no momentum, no inertia, no metric and no field equation** — a pattern in them drifts while something biases its next commitment and stops when that stops, so **nothing here can produce an orbit or a free fall** in the mechanical sense. The limits are set out in [method/models/README.md](method/models/README.md). **The clock well, the self-ordering medium and the age relic need no steering rule**; the drawing-together in finding 3 does, and that rule is ours.

The only other inputs are the Born rule, the area law, 3+1 dimensions, and a starting shape. Everything else here is working out what these imply. The full account: [ontology/Event_Density_An_Ontology.md](ontology/Event_Density_An_Ontology.md).

## The results

### Results from analysis and proof

*These apply known mathematics and physics to ED's own claims; none is new mathematics. ED's contribution is the connection. Read these first.*

* **1. ED's conservation laws fix three numbers that causal dynamical triangulations tunes by hand.** CDT builds spacetime from blocks stacked in time-slices and leaves three totals free — the corner points N₀ and the two kinds of block N₄₁ and N₃₂ — tuned through κ₀, Δ and κ₄. **ED's conserved budgets fix all three**: its event budget fixes N₀, its link budget fixes N₄₁, and conserving forward links fixes N₃₂ through an exact identity, N₁ᵀ = 2N₀ + N₃₂/2. Exact algebra, no new free parameters. In 2+1 dimensions the point they fix sits inside the phase where space does not collapse; in 3+1 it has been measured and lies outside that phase — ED needs a vertex density of 0.044, and CDT's own value there is about 0.075 and never below 0.073 anywhere scanned.

* **2. Below three dimensions, clocks cannot keep time together.** The coupling needed to hold a pattern's clocks together rises without limit in one and two dimensions, and at three and above rises at most very slowly, with a large majority staying locked. It reads the dimension, not the number of connections. So ED's own content rules out one- and two-dimensional worlds.

* **3. Handedness cannot be written into mirror-symmetric rules.** A proved theorem, checked by a script here: symmetric rules give exactly zero drift. If the world has a handedness — and it does — its state picked it, not its laws.

* **4. ED carries the dimension it is given.** Three-plus-one is a declared primitive; measured with ED's own definition of dimension, the rules carry a dimension they are given and add none of their own. With results 2 and 5 it is bounded to three, conditionally.

* **5. If a particle is an uncuttable knot, three is the only dimension that works.** Loops cannot knot in two dimensions, and every knot comes undone in four or more; only in three do uncuttable loops come in many kinds that last. With result 2, that bounds the number from both sides: a reason for three, conditional on what a particle is, not a derivation of space. **What makes such a knot uncuttable in ED is that an uncommitted link's place is held for its own pair**, by identities fixed when those places came into being; models show the knot comes apart quickly without it. The same reservation keeps a knot its own kind while it travels through grown, blinking space, where without it the kind changes in every run.

### Results from models

*What ED's own ingredients do, mostly in supplied three-dimensional space: behaviours of the rules, not claims about nature.*

ED declares three-plus-one dimensions as an input; it does not claim to make space. So the models supply space as a 3D grid and ask what ED's own ingredients do in it.

With space supplied as the ontology declares, ED's own ingredients — flow, clocks slowed by committed matter, commitment, dissolution after ħ, new places being born, a spending budget, polarity — were run to see what they do. Among the results:

* **Structure has a switch**, derived on paper and found exactly at the predicted value, with nothing tuned.
* **Structure is temporary**: uniform, then structure, then uniform again, ended from outside by new places being born or from inside by spending.
* **The only memory is the present state**: a pattern is wherever its events are. Remove them and it is gone; move them and it follows. Nothing stores where it was.
* **Patterns host, merge, bind and part**, and polarity is selected by the surroundings.
* **A medium orders itself**: under the ontology's commitment rule, the whole medium comes to share one phase that nothing supplied — and at the event layer the mechanism is identified (result 21): what holds places together is that **a commitment is something two places do together**, so committing pulls a pair into agreement.
* **Where a new event goes costs little** once space is given.
* **When the rate of commitment is the clock**, the switch stays exactly in place, and structure is ended mainly from outside.
* **Space is a necessary input**: nine models tried to grow space from ED's other ingredients and none did, so the dimension and arrangement of space are inputs, not outputs — and what the input adds is **the dimension of a neighbour relation the ontology already supplies**, which four further candidate rules failed to fix from ED's other ingredients. Asked from the other side, **possibility dissolving and re-forming under ED's own rules heals a structure's bottlenecks but cannot hold a dimension even when given one**, because every re-formed link is a shortcut.
* **Local three-dimensional order can be made from disorder, but not kept as space grows**: ED's ingredients grow a near-cubic glass, right on average and wrong in detail, and nothing tried crystallises it.
* **ED's rules don't spoil space they are given, and it can be built up place by place**: with which pairs may be neighbours supplied, ED's own joining rules decide which pairs actually link — and **places arriving one at a time leave a network that stays open, three-dimensional and crossable**, in every arrival order tried. **Open** means no narrow way through: the tightest cut separating a region from the rest stays at 167–174 links and does not narrow as the region grows, so nothing ends up behind a door. **The maze comes from one rule in particular** — letting a new place link only where it closes a square, which leaves places stranded on a single link for ever, and a single link is a door. The ruled rule also survives ED's own dissolution and comes out with **more** links than it grew with, while the square-only rule decays under it, since a dissolved link can only be won back across a square.
* **A region can be cut off two ways, by breaking its links or by stopping its clock, and the clock wins** — and *why* it wins is the result. **Breaking links undoes itself:** a sharp, mass-sized surface of zero bandwidth does form while the region is fed from outside, but once the seal cuts off its own supply, possibility stops arriving, commitment stops, the drain stops, **and the links heal open.** Letting the mass grow does not rescue the seal; it undoes it faster. **Slowing the clock does not undo itself**, because it isolates a region by starving its dealings with the outside rather than by tearing the network: as the mass grows the clock slows without limit and with no floor, as a clean power of time. **But nothing is ever cut off** — a slowed-clock trap is a well, not a seal, so a region gets ever closer to being isolated and never arrives.

### Results from the event layer

*ED says becoming happens in discrete commitments, each occupying a place for a while before dissolving back into possibility, with a clock at a place counting its own commitments. The nineteen model results above run on continuous amounts — the coarse-grained picture, the ontology's own "shadow." These six model the events themselves.*

* **The One Being under scarcity settles and stays settled.** The One Being is the state ED says to start from: **possibility spread evenly, every place in step, nothing anywhere distinguished from anywhere else** — and *becoming* is what breaks that evenness, each commitment carving a local difference out of a uniform background. Here places share a budget that cannot serve all of them at once, and the result is neither a frozen structure nor a scatter: one coherent medium, blink rates **more even than chance**, a steady difference of about two blinks between neighbours, and nothing starved.
* **What keeps the medium coherent is that committing is something two places do together.** Because a commitment is mutual, each one pulls both places toward agreement — which is what stops the medium drifting apart into noise. Controls separate the candidates: take that pull away and coherence collapses; take away the phase-weighted choice of partner and nothing is lost. **Whom a place commits with barely matters; that it commits *with* someone is the mechanism.**
* **The arrow of time comes out as the ontology states it.** All the possibility piled into a ball and the rest of space left quiet: becoming spreads, the gradient flattens, and within a few hundred ticks the start is forgotten in every rate — matching a uniform start to three decimals. **One trace never relaxes: the place where becoming began ends permanently older, by about 5 blinks**, which is time as the ontology defines it, accumulated and unrepayable. The lead is permanent at every speed of possibility; its size depends on how long possibility has to spread.
* **Possibility is the only budget**, and where it is plentiful the ceiling on becoming is ħ alone — a place cannot commit again until its commitment dissolves.
* **Matter slows the clocks around it**, read raw with no correction: slow where matter sits, then halving shell by shell across six shells to the background, with the same sign at every seed, box size and density.
* **The shortage behind that well takes the Newtonian shape** in a rich medium, matching the exact solution for a drain of that size to 0.7%. **The shape is what any conserved thing spreading in three dimensions does; what is ED's is the sink.** And reach and strength turn out to be two faces of one quantity, so neither can be made large without losing the other.

## Check it yourself

**The handedness theorem:**

```
python results/Handedness/check_result.py
```

Needs Python with numpy. It tests the theorem for up to six lanes, for random mirrors, and for hops reaching several places at once, plus two edge cases.

**The event layer — results 20–25:**

```
python method/models/run_event_layer.py
```

Needs Python with numpy and scipy. One self-contained script, the final rules and nothing else, which prints each published figure beside the one it has just measured. `medium`, `arrow` or `well` runs one benchmark on its own; `--quick` gives a faster, smaller look. **The steady medium takes under a minute; all three take about ten.** The arrow and the well average two seeds, each read against its own matched control, and print the per-seed values beside the mean. What is ED's and what is the model's is in the script's own header.

Model results 1–12 and the clock floor can be rerun from [method/models/](method/models/) (Python with numpy and scipy); its README lists the script and command for each. The scripts for results 13–18 are held with the working record and available on request.

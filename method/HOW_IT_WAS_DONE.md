# How it was done

*Allen Proxmire, 2026-09-27. The practices behind every result in this repository. The standing rules are in [STANDARDS.md](STANDARDS.md); the code is in [models/](models/).*

---

## The two phases

**Analysis and proof (September 13–24).** ED's primitives were turned into exact rules and tested against three questions: does ED's structure constrain an established theory of quantum spacetime; does its requirement that clocks keep time together restrict the shape of space; and can its rules produce three-dimensional space rather than be given it. Fourteen model builds, each self-contained. Results: [../results/RESULTS.md](../results/RESULTS.md).

**Models in supplied space (September 24–27).** With three-plus-one dimensions taken as the ontology declares them, a 3D grid was supplied and ED's own ingredients — flow, clock slowing by committed matter, commitment above a threshold, dissolution after ħ, expansion, spending, polarity — were run in it to see what they do. Seventeen models, each built from a written card. Results: [../results/MODEL_RESULTS.md](../results/MODEL_RESULTS.md).

## The practices

**Meanings are decided by the author, in writing, before anything is built.** What a term of the ontology means in a model — distance, the One Being, memory, ħ, where new places are born, whether committing runs on the local clock — is settled by the person whose ontology it is, and recorded as a decision with its date. A default proposed and not objected to is not a decision.

**Each model starts from a card.** The card states the model, where each rule comes from in the ontology, what will be measured, and what each possible outcome would mean — written and reviewed before the code.

**What will be measured is fixed before the run; what counts as success is not.** Observables are chosen in advance so they cannot be picked to fit. Verdicts are not, because a success criterion chosen before a system is understood steers the work toward the wrong target. Where a result needs a threshold — how big a difference counts as real — the threshold is written down before the data is read.

**Derive first where algebra can answer.** The structure switch was derived on paper and then found at the predicted value. When a later rule change reached into it, the algebra was redone before any run.

**Every instrument is calibrated on known answers**, including a null case, before it is used on anything unknown. Dimension measures were checked on a ring (1), a flat grid (2), a cubic grid (3) and a random web (nothing). Model code reproduces the earlier model exactly before a new ingredient is added.

**Controls are built to match.** A filter that rejects commitments by phase is compared with one that rejects exactly as often while ignoring phase. A rule for where new space appears is compared with others removing exactly the same total, to one part in 10¹⁵. A weak pattern living inside a strong one is compared with the weak pattern alone.

**Seeds and resolution are stated.** Results are read across at least two seeds where they matter, and every lifetime is quoted at the resolution it was sampled at.

**Three layers are kept apart.** Observations (measured), conclusions (follow from observations by arithmetic or calibration), and interpretations (need a reading). An interpretation can be replaced by a better one; an observation changes only if an instrument was wrong.

**Two independent working sessions reviewed each other.** One built and ran the models; the other wrote cards and reviewed results before anything was recorded as a finding. Each result here passed that review.

**The published literature was checked** before anything was claimed — for CDT's identities and measured values, for the physics of coupled clocks, and for the complexity and clumping results the models resemble.

**Tuned settings are labelled.** Any number chosen to make something work is marked as chosen, and by whom. Knobs are allowed; what matters is whether a knob does work and which layer it belongs to — the ontology, or the rules that operate it.

## Scope, stated with each result

Every result carries its strength (proved, derived and confirmed, observed, estimated) and its scope (grid size, settings, seeds). The models show what ED's ingredients do in supplied space; they are not derivations of space, gravity or cosmology, and they claim nothing about nature beyond the resemblances they note.

**One instrument lesson worth carrying to any similar work:** how distance grows with size cannot, on its own, tell three-dimensional space from a critical tangle — both read about one-third — so it must always be read alongside a measure of shape.
